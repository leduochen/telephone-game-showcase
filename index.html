One message. Ten retellings.

Follow real speech as it passes through a voice model, one retelling at a time. Compare audio input with text input, and listen across three independent runs.

Data sources

50 English recordings: 15 audiobook clips from LibriSpeech, 17 parliamentary speeches from VoxPopuli, 12 human dialogue turns from Audio MultiChallenge, and 6 meeting clips from AMI. Clips are 5–25 seconds long and total about 12 minutes.

Source preparation

Collected on November 18, 2026, using published continuous segments. Mono PCM audio was resampled to 24 kHz for the experiment, without gain normalization, trimming, or speed changes. The source text shown below is the frozen independent ASR transcript; the publisher transcript is available separately.

Experiment setup

Four audio-input conditions and two text-input conditions. Each run starts from the same source (H0); every hop uses a fresh session of the same model. In audio conditions, the previous output audio becomes the next input. In text conditions, its independent ASR transcript becomes the next input. All conditions produce speech.
